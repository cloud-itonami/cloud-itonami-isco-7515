# Business Model: Food and Beverage Tasters and Graders Sample-Intake Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7515`
- ISCO-08: `7515`
- Occupation: Food and Beverage Tasters and Graders
- Social impact: food-safety, quality-assurance, consumer-protection

## Customer

- food and beverage quality-control labs / grading houses
- independent taster/grader crews / tasting-panel cooperatives

## Offer

- taster/session shift scheduling coordination
- sample-intake/batch-identifier record logging
- tasting-supply order coordination (palate cleansers, sample containers)
- sample-condition anomaly surfacing to the human taster

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no performance of the sensory evaluation itself, ever — no robot can
  taste
- no direct recording or finalization of a quality-grade assignment,
  ever
- no direct recording or finalization of a pass/fail determination,
  ever
- no finalization or authorization of a food-safety clearance, ever
- flagged sample-condition anomalies (visible defect, off-odor
  reported by intake staff) always route to human sign-off, regardless
  of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
